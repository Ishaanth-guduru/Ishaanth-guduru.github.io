My Story

I'm Ishaanth Guduru, an Electrical Engineering student at Georgia Tech from New York. I got into engineering by building things: a differential-drive robot that navigates a course using a PID control loop, and a replica of the Boss DS-1 guitar distortion pedal that I breadboarded and then soldered onto a custom PCB. Each project taught me that the real learning happens when something doesn't work and you have to debug it. These interests meet in Analog Integrated Circuit Engineering, where circuitry takes over. Outside of class, I play tennis and guitar.
