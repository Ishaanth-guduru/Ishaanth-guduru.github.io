My Story

I'm Ishaanth Guduru, an Electrical Engineering student at Georgia Tech from New York. I got into engineering by building things: a differential-drive robot that navigates a course using a PID control loop, and a replica of the Boss DS-1 guitar distortion pedal that I breadboarded and then soldered onto a custom PCB. Each project taught me that the real learning happens when something doesn't work and you have to debug it. Alongside hardware, I've always been fascinated by financial markets, which led me to build a Python swing trading bot. Those two interests meet in FPGA engineering, where hardware speed directly decides outcomes, and that's the career I'm working toward. Outside of class, I play tennis and guitar, lift, and train for triathlons.
